# Attributions

## City data

`src/data/worldCities.json` is derived from [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/) (`cities15000`, `admin1CodesASCII`, `countryInfo`), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Used to power the location autocomplete on the profile edit form -- trimmed to just `"City, State"` / `"City, Country"` display labels (population ≥ 15,000), no other GeoNames fields retained.
